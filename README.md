# Elvis Guitar

A personal practice app for learning Elvis Presley songs on acoustic guitar.

- **Tuner** — uses your microphone, needle shows cents sharp/flat, auto or locked string.
- **Starter path** — nine short lessons for a brand-new player, with a one-minute chord-change drill.
- **Songs** — 14 songs, easiest first, each with a beginner version (open chords) and a record version, chord diagrams, strum pattern, play-along with backing guitar and click, an original practice lick, and progress tracking.
- **Lyrics** — full lyrics for public-domain songs (Amazing Grace, Santa Lucia, Danny Boy). For copyrighted songs, paste your own lyrics with `[G]` chord markers; they're saved on your device only.

Everything is plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. Progress is stored in the browser's localStorage. Works offline after the first visit and can be added to your home screen.

Chord charts are simplified study versions — trust your ear against the records.
